# MedClub V0.9 design direction

## Concept: the clinical rounds board

Recruitment is treated as a transparent working process rather than a marketing page. The opening screen combines a dark medical signal field with warm, tactile role cards and a visible six-stage path. Students can understand the role, commitment and next stage before giving information.

## Signature behavior

- The hero signal uses quiet rings, nodes and a heartbeat rhythm without literal clinical imagery.
- Position cards have team-specific accents, asymmetric curves and small “signal” details.
- Hover movement is brief and restrained; active navigation icons scan and pulse.
- Application stages connect visually so the current step is never hidden in a status badge alone.
- Onboarding progress and volunteer hours feel operational, not decorative.
- V0.8 authentication remains the only WebGL surface; V0.9 is CSS-only.

## UX rules

- Public role facts remain readable without signing in.
- Signed-out application actions preserve a safe internal return path.
- Draft and submit are separate actions; screening never starts implicitly.
- Closed roles remain readable but cannot accept a draft.
- Student-facing status history is chronological and specific.
- Membership, symbolic titles and permissions are visibly separated.
- All device-local behavior carries an honest preview notice.

## Accessibility and responsive rules

- Form controls have stable IDs and concise accessible names.
- Recruitment navigation and team tabs hide native scrollbars without disabling scroll.
- Touch targets remain large and text inputs use at least 16px text on mobile.
- Six workflow stages reflow to three and then two columns instead of overflowing.
- Logical properties support RTL and directional arrows mirror where necessary.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes orbit, pulse and lift animations.
