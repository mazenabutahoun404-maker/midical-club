# V0.5 visual direction — clinical signal

V0.5 avoids generic hospital-blue templates and literal medical decoration. The medical identity comes from behavior and information structure:

- Short scan-line route reveals.
- Double-pulse icon response on hover and active states.
- Signal traces that communicate activity and continuity.
- Structured authorship and scientific-review metadata.
- Red/russet surfaces with dark diagnostic panels.
- Soft asymmetric corners rather than rigid dashboard rectangles.
- Motion disabled or reduced when the operating system requests it.

The media archive intentionally uses designed placeholders. Fabricated photographs would weaken trust. Approved club photography should arrive through the future CMS with contributor, source and rights records.
