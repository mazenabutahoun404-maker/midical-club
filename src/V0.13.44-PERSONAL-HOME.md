# MedClub V0.13.44 — personal home and balanced Arabic reader

This is the complete `src` folder, based on V0.13.43. Replace your existing `src` folder and keep the existing project configuration and dependencies. No dependency was added. All previous source files are retained.

## Arabic PDF spacing

The course reader now defines equal physical left and right frame margins, including Arabic RTL, and clears obsolete RTL side-index margins on the study shell. Focus mode also uses a symmetric frame. This prevents older navigation rules from leaving an extra gutter on the right. The large-screen baseline was already balanced in the reproduced configuration; the change explicitly removes the conflicting legacy margins.

## My Space

- A next-action hero replaces the old personal home. An upcoming exam within 24 hours takes priority; otherwise it resumes the most recently opened study resource and its saved PDF page, or provides a clear first step into assigned courses.
- Students can save their next exam title, course and date. The form validates future dates and displays Amman time. Only an authenticated live calendar or the student's saved reminder supplies exam urgency; the illustrative demo calendar does not trigger it.
- Quick revision opens a course-specific kit with saved materials prioritized, imported PDFs, bookmarked pages and course notes. Bookmarks and notes link to the exact PDF page without opening the note editor. The kit remains usable if optional note data fails and reports the failure.
- Three pinned shortcuts remain in a stable order until the student edits them. The shortcuts offer courses, library, recall, notes, calendar, focus and offline packs.
- The remaining home sections show actual upcoming plans, recall cards, the existing 3D course books, recent studying saves and reviewed-resource progress. There is no invented exam-readiness score.
- English and Arabic use mirrored hero layouts. Phone layouts put the main study action before the campus. Actions have generous touch targets; the revision kit moves keyboard focus to its heading. The old desktop sidebar margin transition is disabled on the personal home's phone layout to prevent temporary horizontal overflow when resizing.

The saved reminder, pinned shortcuts and campus milestones are private to the account on this device. These additions do not introduce a new server persistence contract. The separately built exam platform is intentionally not integrated yet; its source is the next input.

## Your growing campus

The six buildings keep the established campus composition. A clean, quiet starting scene becomes furnished and active through a fixed restored photograph revealed in building regions. The same restored pixels remain mounted, so developing another building does not regenerate completed buildings. Only alpha-mask edges are softened; the source photographs are not blurred by the animation.

Each building has four stages: ready, in use, growing and established. Progress uses distinct evidence:

| Building | Evidence | Milestone thresholds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| My courses | Completed reviews of assigned catalogue resources | 1, 5, 15 |
| Recall | Different recall cards reviewed at least once | 1, 5, 15 |
| My library | Saved assigned resources or organized collections with at least three valid saved items | 1, 5, 12 saves; 1, 2, 3 collections |
| Community | Verified activity/volunteering claims and finished study rooms the student joined | 1, 3, 6 |
| Mentorship | Accepted mentorship requests | 1, 3, 6 |
| Contribution | Approved or published contributions | 1, 3, 6 |

Completing 6, 18 or 36 distinct resource/card reviews can also develop the community, mentorship and contribution buildings, so social participation is optional. Repeat reviews of the same card do not multiply that count. Visits and timer minutes do not count. Earned stages persist when records are removed or the student takes a break. These are motivational visual milestones, not academic proficiency measurements.

The buildings link to their personal destinations, with ordinary text links as a touch and image-failure fallback. Short path and light sequences use the existing visible-minute scheduler, stop while the page is hidden or outside the viewport, and respect reduced motion. The campus stays quiet when the exam hero is active.
