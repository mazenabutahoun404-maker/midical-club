## Console diagnosis

The installed Vite client contains the exact `[vite] connecting...` and `[vite] connected.` lines as debug logging. The installed development React DOM bundle contains the DevTools invitation. These are normal development output.

The reported failed URLs are on `pro.similarweb.com` and `secure.similarweb.com`, inside the Similarweb sales extension. No Similarweb integration, company-domain query or extension-runtime messaging exists in MedClub's TypeScript/JavaScript source. Changing this application's API or CORS settings cannot repair the extension's remote login redirect.

To remove those failures from the local development session: open Chrome's Extensions menu, choose Manage Extensions, turn off Similarweb, and reload localhost. A separate extension-free browser profile is another option. This update does not hide errors or monkey-patch console/fetch behavior.

The standalone `Could not establish connection. Receiving end does not exist` line does not include a script URL. It is consistent with extension messaging, but its exact origin cannot be established from this text alone.

## Homepage recommendation — discussion only

The homepage is unchanged in this release. Repeated destinations, multiple large decorative scenes and abstract headings weaken the hierarchy. Get involved is emphasized as requested; the assumption that it will be the most visited destination needs usage evidence.

Recommended next structure:
1. Preserve the interactive campus hero as the main visual introduction.
2. Replace the large curiosity composition with compact study shortcuts: Courses, Exams, Study tools and Saved library.
3. Keep the library illustration and real course books as the other large visual feature. Lead with the literal heading Courses.
4. Combine Events and Opportunities into one Get involved area: one featured event, compact upcoming dates and clear opportunity deadlines. Use a calm themed surface and useful event media instead of another decorative building scene.
5. Present the Medical Journal as an editorial spread: one leading article and two compact article rows, with relevant article imagery when available.
6. Finish with a concise Community and Mentorship area focused on people and two clear actions. Remove the repeated six-building destination map from this section.

Use one restrained reveal pattern across those sections. Let the existing campus paths and book interactions provide the distinctive motion.
