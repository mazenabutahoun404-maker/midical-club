# MedClub V0.13.39 — Campus night scene and Arabic composition

This complete source folder builds on V0.13.38 and applies the four requested homepage hero fixes. The old dark side overlay concealed parts of Learning and Journals; dark mode now uses a dedicated night artwork with all six buildings visible.

## Requested changes

- Light modes keep the accepted day artwork. Dark modes use the same campus composition at night, with illuminated interiors, paths and globe. The selected appearance mode and the device's System setting choose the appropriate responsive image.
- Arabic places the campus on the left and the copy on the right. The image, building hit areas and light traces mirror together; destination labels counter-mirror to remain readable and aligned with the plaques. Small screens retain the stacked layout and all six labeled touch links.
- The empty copy space and outer artwork edges fade gradually into the current page background. The broad side overlay and darkening filter are removed from the default campus artwork.
- Hover and keyboard-focus traces use the current theme's `--ring` accent through `--campus-path-color`, including live theme changes.

All six route targets, search, pointer motion, reduced-motion behavior, custom artwork and failed-image fallbacks remain supported. Image failure is tracked by source so changing between day and night can recover independently.

Change only the lighting and background from daytime to a beautiful calm nighttime scene. Preserve the exact camera, crop, aspect ratio 1672:941, and every building, globe, staircase, path, tree, person, and blank signboard at precisely the same position and scale as the input. This is used with coordinate-mapped interactive links; moving architecture or blank plaques is forbidden. All six buildings must remain fully visible, including the open-book auditorium at upper left and the journal building at lower left. Keep the original generous empty space on the left, but change it to smooth deep charcoal navy night background, softly illuminated near the campus, darkening gently outward. Use cool moonlit architectural highlights with warm illuminated windows, warm subtle path lighting and a softly glowing glass globe. Retain the cream stone, burgundy materials, and original luxurious detailed 3D architectural style; their colors naturally adapt to night. The whole campus should remain clear rather than dim or obscured. Preserve ALL blank plaques as blank cream surfaces. No text, no labels, no lettering, no logos, no stars, no moon, no new objects, no haze across the buildings, no black side panel, no framing, no layout redesign. Night lighting only. Exact same composition; output wide 1672 by 941 or the closest same-aspect high-resolution landscape.
