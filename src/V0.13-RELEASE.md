# MedClub V0.13 — Learning insights and content health

V0.13 adds evidence-aware analytics and academic reflection on top of the complete V0.12.1 source. It does not replace the V0.12.1 notebook, navigation behavior, course materials, public site, personal tools, Admin/CMS, or governance workflows.

## New routes

- `/admin/analytics` — aggregate engagement trends, learning funnel, content demand, range filters and CSV export.
- `/admin/content-health` — explainable file, reviewer, reference, translation, rights and freshness checks with filters and CSV export.
- `/my/progress` — private course activity signals, self-rated confidence, practice-result history and a simple next-step suggestion.

## Data boundaries

- Admin analytics are deterministic illustrative aggregates. They are labeled as sample data and never presented as live student telemetry.
- Personal confidence and practice attempts are scoped to the authenticated student ID and stored in browser-local preview storage.
- Personal insight data is not exposed through the Admin analytics or content-health interfaces.
- Confidence is explicitly self-rated. Practice results are entered by the student and are not treated as official grades.
- The next-step suggestion is a transparent sort over local confidence and reviewed-resource counts. It does not claim to diagnose mastery.
- Content health checks completeness and freshness only. It does not automatically certify scientific accuracy.
- CSV cells are quoted and spreadsheet-formula prefixes are neutralized.

## Architecture and performance

- `InsightsService` is part of the central service registry and can be replaced by a consent-aware HTTP adapter.
- V0.13 screens are lazy-loaded at the route boundary.
- Charts use small native SVG and CSS components; no charting or 3D dependency was added.
- Desktop, tablet and phone layouts are covered by responsive styles.
- Arabic RTL, keyboard controls and reduced-motion behavior are retained.

## Production boundary

Before production, replace illustrative analytics with privacy-reviewed aggregate events, define retention and consent rules, enforce minimum cohort sizes, connect authorized content metadata, and move personal records to account-backed storage if synchronization is desired.

## Verification

The V0.13 suite covers range aggregation, content-health checks, account isolation, input validation, CSV safety, routing, English/Arabic parity, responsive/reduced-motion CSS, filters, confidence changes and practice logging. The complete inherited V0.1–V0.12.1 regression suite remains part of the release gate. Final verification completed with 190/190 checks, strict TypeScript and the production build passing.
