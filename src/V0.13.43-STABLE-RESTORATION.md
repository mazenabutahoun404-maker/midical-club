# MedClub V0.13.43 — stable building restoration and arrival stars

This complete source folder builds on V0.13.42 and changes the registration campus restoration only. No dependency was added. Replace your project's existing `src` folder and retain its configuration and dependencies.

## Why details were changing

V0.13.42 changed the entire scene to another independently generated image after each milestone. Those images redrew small details in buildings that had already been repaired. The intermediate images remain in the source archive, but registration no longer switches between them.

The updated renderer keeps the awaiting photograph and one fixed restored photograph mounted. Each completed field reveals its building's region from that same restored photograph. Previously completed buildings keep their original restored pixels through all later milestones. Soft alpha edges blend the regions; the photographs themselves are not blurred, transformed by the animation, or crossfaded. Completing the last field exposes the whole restored scene, including the remaining ground.

## Arrival effect

The renderer waits for the path's actual stroke transition to finish, including when reaching Create Account changes the path speed. It then restores the building and emits a finite burst at the exact path endpoint:

- Eighteen stars expand radially, with alternating sizes and short comet tails.
- A central star flashes briefly with two expanding light rings.
- Highlights combine warm light with the current theme's path color.
- Each burst finishes independently, so continuing to type does not truncate it. At most three bursts coexist; finished SVG nodes and timers are removed.

The existing shared path animation and home/sign-in effects are retained. The new starburst belongs to registration restoration. English/Arabic use the same scene coordinates and mirrored artwork. Phone artwork has its own aligned endpoint and reveal-region map.

Invalidating a field removes its restored region and hides any corresponding arrival burst. Out-of-order completion remains supported. Reduced motion keeps restoration functional and suppresses the fireworks. Hidden pages clear active bursts. Source photographs remain sharp and fixed.
