# MedClub V0.13.18 — Study layout repair

Based on the complete V0.13.17 source. All existing routes, course files,
PDF storage, permissions, notes, GPA, reading-position restoration and other
previous features remain included.

## Changes

- Restored the shelf silhouette and a definite viewport-height desk. The book
  fills the remaining reading area instead of sizing itself to short content.
- Kept the existing main-navigation browsing behavior: it stays visible on
  course pages during scrolling; Study focus removes it and its reveal zone.
- Course sections default to horizontal while browsing and vertical in focus.
  The orientation control switches directly in one press and saves the choice.
- Paper flags bend gently inward and flatten on hover/focus. Preview sheets are
  anchored to the originating flag. Phones expose readable labels without hover.
- Shelf controls share one grid row. The mobile controls button is hidden on
  desktop; smaller screens keep the appearance/study controls in one compact group.
- Removed PDF upload from study tools. Authorized course-section import and its
  existing drag, progress and success feedback remain available.
- The movable toolbar docks after being dragged to an edge, leaving a small
  handle. Hover, tap or keyboard focus restores it; Escape tucks it again and
  Home on the grip restores its usual position. Open panels remain available.
  The timer stays visible on the handle. Invisible tools do not cover the PDF.
- Reduced the wide image wash and tightened the local text backdrop in page
  heroes. Current colors and typography are retained.
