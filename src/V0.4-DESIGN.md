# V0.4 design language

V0.4 makes discovery feel active without turning routine navigation into a spectacle.

## Motion rules

- The welcome veil is short, pointer-transparent and shown once per browser tab.
- Active icons use a one-shot arrival animation; they do not pulse forever.
- Hover motion is small enough to preserve target stability.
- Cards lift and their internal geometry responds, while text stays readable.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all animation through the shared accessibility layer.

## Visual metaphors

- Events use ticket notches, capacity threads and an orbiting campus constellation.
- Opportunities use a door/portal sculpture, deadline orbits and verified-information cards.
- Red marks action and community energy; russet and warm neutrals carry most surfaces.
- Large organic radii continue the modern V0.2/V0.3 direction instead of returning to rigid rectangles.

## UX boundaries

- Save and register are visually and verbally separate.
- Filters remain native controls with explicit labels.
- Active tabs use `aria-pressed`; navigation uses `aria-current`.
- Mobile filters stack and tab rows scroll horizontally rather than shrinking targets.
- Disabled application actions explain what is missing instead of pretending to work.
