# MedClub V0.13.38 — Interactive Living Campus hero

This complete source folder builds on V0.13.37 and implements the selected Living Campus concept. The update is limited to the homepage hero, its default copy, supporting assets and release metadata.

## Appearance and interaction

The approved campus artwork supplies the buildings, globe, materials and lighting. The headline, buttons and destination labels are live HTML/SVG text. The artwork was cleaned of its embedded interface text so these elements remain sharp and can translate to Arabic.

Each building is a real router link:

| Destination | Route |
| --- | --- |
| Learning | `/learn` |
| Media | `/community/media` |
| Community | `/community` |
| Mentorship | `/mentorship` |
| Contribution | `/contribute` |
| Journals | `/journal` |

- Hover or keyboard focus highlights the destination, draws a light trace from the globe and reveals a short description.
- Fine mouse pointers produce a small depth movement. Image and link geometry move together. Motion uses queued pointer updates, a cached rectangle and no idle animation loop; it resets offscreen and respects reduced motion and coarse pointers.
- Clicks, keyboard Enter and modifier clicks use normal link behavior without an artificial exit delay.
- Layout responds to the available content width. Narrow screens show a crop around the six buildings plus ordinary labeled links with at least 48px height. Arabic text keeps the artwork's composition intact.
- Appearance colors come from the current theme. Dark themes use a readable copy area and light plaque backings; Sage, Espresso and Midnight also have restrained scene tints.
- Search remains available from the hero footer.
- Existing custom artwork uploads remain supported. Their geometry is unknown, so they use the ordinary destination links instead of the default campus map. If the image fails to load, the headline, actions and destination links remain usable.

The buildings are part of the artwork. Updating their architecture requires updating the artwork and corresponding coordinates. The interactive controls and effects are live SVG/HTML.

## Artwork preparation

Prepared with the built-in image-generation editing tool, then encoded as the bundled WebP assets above. The production edit prompt was:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached approved MedClub Living Campus hero mockup. Prepare this exact artwork for production as a text-free image, keeping the exact composition and camera, original 16:9 aspect ratio and entire framing.
>
> CHANGE ONLY THESE: remove the left-side MedClub wordmark, both headline lines, the small sentence, the entire red Explore MedClub button including its arrow, the secondary link, and the lower-left "01 — Living Campus" caption. Fill all these cleared areas with the existing continuous warm ivory background and the same subtle floor lighting. Remove the six English destination words from the physical plaque signs on the buildings (Learning, Media, Community, Mentorship, Contribution, Journals), leaving the original ivory plaque faces completely blank, with their original shapes, perspective and shading intact.
>
> PRESERVE EVERYTHING ELSE: all six buildings and their exact positions, proportions, openings, interiors, warm wine-red and ivory materials, reflections, fine gold details, the central glass globe and its position, all trees, people, paths and their curves, steps, the base, sky, floor, shadows and lighting. Do not add or move anything. Do not redesign or simplify the architecture. In particular, preserve the blank plaque positions so live HTML/SVG text can be put exactly on them. Do not change the backdrop color, lighting style or overall crop. Output only the cleaned artwork, no UI, no text, no logos, no annotations.
