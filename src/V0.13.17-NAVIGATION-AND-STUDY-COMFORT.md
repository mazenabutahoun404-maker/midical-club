# MedClub V0.13.17 — Navigation and study comfort

Replace the complete `src/` directory with this source tree. V0.13.16 is the baseline. No original source file is deleted, no runtime dependency is added, and existing routes, PDF permissions/storage, student notes, timer, GPA calculator, saving and page-turn behavior remain included.

## Navigation and course shelf

- Navigation hover controls occupy a fixed tray instead of changing the tab row's width. The tabs and decorative bindings keep their positions when pin/close controls appear. Pin and close remain visible to keyboard users and touch devices.
- The horizontal main navigation has a consistent height and an 8px sticky-section offset. Its tab row can scroll horizontally on smaller desktop/tablet widths; the Learn menu remains outside that scroll container.
- Three restrained inset edge shadows suggest layered paper without enlarging the navigation. The rounded inset sidebar and its existing fold direction are retained. An unnecessary navigation backdrop blur is removed.
- The course shelf is 58px high and slightly wider. The course name sits beside the book row instead of above it. The shelf lip belongs to, and is clipped by, the books' window. Short paper controls remain attached to the top edge with full 44px hit areas and the existing delayed tooltips.
- The current course projects farther from the shelf and has a visible ribbon and edge. Other books still pull out on mouse hover or keyboard focus. Touch devices expose readable book titles directly with 48px visible book surfaces.

## Separate study navigation

Ordinary course browsing keeps campus navigation visible and reserves its space. Scrolling inside the reader does not make it retreat or reveal. Explicit manual hiding remains available. Study focus removes campus navigation and its reveal hint completely; exiting restores ordinary browsing.

Course section flags use the book's top edge while browsing and its side in Study focus. The **Sections** selector in the movable study tool strip provides Automatic, Horizontal and Vertical layouts. Automatic follows the focus state; an explicit choice remains in force in either mode and is saved per account/course alongside the existing reading state. Older stored reading state defaults to Automatic without discarding resource pages, scroll positions or split view.

Horizontal flags have visible names, a scrollable strip on phones, attached previews, keyboard navigation and the existing close/reveal behavior. The section rail and campus-navigation hint no longer share an edge in ordinary browsing. On phones, the movable tools are constrained above the visible campus navigation; focus returns that space to study.

## Dark appearance

The logo and light palette are retained. Dark surfaces use neutral warm plum with muted wine actions, soft white ink and distinct teal, blue, sage, amber and lilac accents. The navigation gradient has close tonal steps instead of a red-to-black transition. Older dark surface aliases are also adjusted, so legacy panels use the same calmer surface family. Two opaque legacy inks below 4.5:1 on the raised neutral surface were lifted. GPA results and hero eyebrow text use the readable accent-ink token rather than the darker button-fill token.

PDF page images and hero images retain their original colors. No image inversion, animated gradient or new media asset is introduced.
