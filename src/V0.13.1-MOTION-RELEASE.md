# MedClub V0.13.1 — Unified motion system

V0.13.1 applies one restrained interaction language across the complete V0.1–V0.13 platform. No route, workflow, data model, role, permission, academic resource, or public-page capability is removed.

## Motion vocabulary

- **Pulse** confirms active navigation, saved items and unread state.
- **Scan** introduces medical surfaces and responsive empty states.
- **Paper** gives the academic notebook believable depth and section changes.
- **Signal** communicates progress through recruitment and version stages.
- **Aperture** remains the transition between public, personal, authentication and Admin spaces.

## Applied improvements

1. Navigation icons respond on hover and perform one active-state pulse.
2. Buttons have a short physical press response.
3. Keyboard focus arrives clearly without replacing the persistent outline.
4. Cards reveal depth and hierarchy with restrained lift and icon movement.
5. The course notebook separates its page stack, bends bookmarks and settles into reading mode.
6. Newly saved items send a temporary bookmark toward the visible Library destination.
7. Completed process connectors fill like a short clinical signal.
8. The hidden-navigation glance brightens and moves toward pointer intent.
9. Academic, medical and Admin routes retain distinct paper, surface and diagnostic entrances.
10. Filtered card collections receive short, capped entrance staggering.
11. Forms provide focused, valid and invalid state responses.
12. Only the first unread notification breathes; read items settle.
13. Calendar entries place like notes and selected dates receive one ink-ring response.
14. Empty states wake once, scan once and then remain still.

## Performance and accessibility constraints

- Interaction motion uses transforms and opacity wherever practical.
- List staggering is capped after the fourth item.
- Pointer-proximity effects run only on fine hover pointers.
- No additional WebGL, image, model or animation dependency was added.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes decorative transitions, flights, paper movement and ambient notification motion.
- Forced-colors mode retains meaningful focus, selection and save feedback.
- The single DOM content tree remains intact during route transitions.
