# MedClub V0.9 integration checklist

## Replace and build

- Back up the current `src` directory, then replace it as one unit.
- Keep the existing dependencies and Vite/Tailwind configuration.
- Run strict TypeScript checking, the full automated suite and the production build.
- Confirm the console has no MedClub source exceptions.

## Public recruitment

- Open `/recruitment` and test search, every team tab and the closed-role switch.
- Open each role and confirm responsibility, requirement, eligibility, commitment and deadline data.
- Confirm closed roles remain readable but cannot accept applications.
- Use keyboard and touch to scroll filters; no native scrollbar should appear.

## Application workflow

- While signed out, start from a role and confirm sign-in returns to that role.
- Save a draft, reload, continue it and explicitly submit it.
- Confirm incomplete forms, duplicate active applications and closed roles are rejected.
- Withdraw a draft/submitted sample and confirm the history stays visible.
- Confirm submitted forms cannot be silently edited.

## Personal workspace

- Review `/my/applications`, every stage filter, interview details and history.
- Toggle onboarding tasks and confirm progress updates.
- Review `/my/team`, simultaneous positions and verified/pending hours.
- Confirm recruitment interviews appear in calendar and relevant updates appear in notifications.
- Confirm public position search results open the correct role.

## Responsive, RTL and accessibility

- Test expanded/collapsed and vertical/horizontal navigation on large screens.
- Test phone bottom navigation and personal “More” destinations.
- Test English and Arabic at 200% zoom and narrow widths.
- Complete role search, application, withdrawal and onboarding with keyboard only.
- Enable reduced motion and confirm pulse/orbit effects become static.
- Check focus restoration when the application and confirmation dialogs close.

## Before production recruitment

- Replace the local adapter with authenticated server endpoints.
- Validate role status, eligibility and all transitions on the server.
- Restrict reviewer access and record reviewer identity/reasons in an immutable audit history.
- Define consent, privacy, retention, deletion, appeal and communication policies.
- Verify volunteer hours server-side and preserve the verifier and evidence.
- Keep team membership, symbolic titles and website capabilities separate.
