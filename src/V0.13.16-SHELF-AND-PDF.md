# MedClub V0.13.16 — Shelf and PDF controls

Replace the entire `src/` directory with this complete source tree. Existing routes, course content, themes, permissions, local PDFs, notes, timer, GPA tools and study-position storage are retained. No dependency was added.

## Course shelf

Outside Study focus, the course shelf is attached to the viewport top with no upper gap. It occupies about three quarters of a desktop screen, with empty margins and a central book area. Its decorative outline tapers to approximately one pixel at both ends; the outline does not clip book links or controls. On narrower screens the shelf uses 10% side margins, a horizontally scrollable book row, Back to courses on one side and Course controls on the other.

The small controls resemble papers tucked into a wallet. About 30% of their paper surface is visible normally, and hover or keyboard focus reveals about 75%. Their actual hit areas remain 44 pixels rather than shrinking to the visible paper. Hover/focus tooltips show after 500 milliseconds, remain while keyboard focus stays, dismiss with Escape and have no native-tooltip duplicate. Touch does not require a preliminary reveal tap.

Appearance, language, Study focus and split view remain available. The compact Course controls panel groups them on phones and tablets, includes visible purpose labels, and closes with Escape or an outside pointer. The duplicate header Study tools button is removed. The movable tool strip at the bottom still provides the timer, notes, GPA and PDF importer, including the live timer badge.

The entire shelf is absent in Study focus. Exiting focus returns keyboard focus to a visible header control. Campus navigation reveal remains absent in focus.

## Sticky paper sections

Section flags emerge from the book edge with a glued edge, slight alternating paper angles, folded corners and themed paper. Hover/focus extends the flag to show its name and first-material glimpse. An open preview keeps the corresponding flag extended. The existing section close/reveal behavior and keyboard navigation remain. Short screens retain vertical section scrolling and an attached preview instead of extending a label beyond the viewport. Arabic mirrors the binding and directional controls.

## Compact PDF controls

Readers narrower than 640 pixels, including split-view companion readers, keep Previous page, page number, Next page and one PDF tools control in a single row. Zoom, search and page bookmarks are grouped into a bounded options drawer with labelled actions. Search opens its field and focuses it immediately. Escape and outside pointer dismiss the drawer; widening the reader restores direct controls without resetting its page or zoom.

On phones the resource title truncates instead of pushing controls outside the reader. Open original file and Close retain 44-pixel targets. Details, Save and Reviewed remain in a quiet footer; Reviewed uses its icon with a full accessible name. The existing Save animation and full-height page-turn edges are retained. No PDF rendering or import authorization was replaced.

## Validation and limits

- 71 unique checks passed across the focused shelf/PDF suite and relevant academic/navigation regression suites.
- Strict TypeScript and the production build passed.
- New PDF menu interaction tests use a controlled renderer fixture to verify zoom, bookmarking, search, resizing and dismissal; existing PDF data/import checks use the established harness.
- Responsive media rules, Arabic mirroring, reduced-motion behavior and forced-colour fallbacks are included. Live browser visual QA was unavailable in this session. Automated DOM checks do not certify real-device layout, clipping, animation smoothness or frame rate.
- The inherited shared-entry bundle-size advisory remains. Course components and PDF rendering remain separately loaded.

PDFs are still stored on the current device. Course imports and removal still require content-review, publishing or builder capabilities; shared publication and server enforcement require the existing backend seam.
