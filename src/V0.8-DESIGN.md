# MedClub V0.8 design direction

## Concept: the identity threshold

Authentication is the transition from the public medical community into a student's private study world. The screen uses two related surfaces: a deep red/russet living medical field and a warm, quiet task surface. The form is visible immediately; spectacle never blocks access.

## Signature visual

The decorative WebGL composition suggests cellular motion, a double helix and connected learning without showing literal clinical imagery. Pointer response is restrained and the palette stays within MedClub red, russet, warm white and ink. Layered curves, light bloom and subtle grid texture replace hard rectangular page structure.

## Motion rules

- Motion communicates entry, focus and account-state changes.
- WebGL remains decorative, capped and pausable.
- Auth forms use short directional transitions instead of long introductions.
- Hover and active feedback follows the existing pulse/scan language.
- Reduced-motion users receive stable opacity and static-field treatments.

## Navigation repair language

Scrollbars are hidden without disabling scrolling. Soft directional shadows reveal more destinations and reverse as the user moves through the rail. The cue is quieter than another button and works in both vertical and horizontal orientations.

## UX guardrails

- The primary account task appears in the first viewport.
- Errors are local, specific and recoverable.
- Password visibility is user-controlled.
- Return paths cannot leave the app origin.
- Recovery does not reveal whether an email exists.
- Browser-local preview boundaries are visible; the interface never claims production security.
