# Decorative heart illustration

Source: **Human Heart 3d Model**, Sourav Pan / Biology Notes Online, NIH 3D entry **3DPX-022787**, version **1.01**.

- Entry: https://3d.nih.gov/entries/22787?version=1.01

- Original source: https://biologynotesonline.com/human-heart-definition-location-anatomy-structure-functions/

MedClub modifications: vertex clustering and normal recalculation; textures removed; warm porcelain material. The transparent WebP poster is an offline rendering of the same simplified mesh. The application loads both assets locally, with no NIH request at runtime.

This is a decorative illustration, not validated clinical anatomy or a diagnostic/educational reference. Appearance, light paths, pulse timing, and field milestones are artistic effects and do not represent physiology or health data. Hosting by NIH does not imply endorsement.
