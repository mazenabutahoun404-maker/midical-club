# MedClub V0.13.50 — illustrated campus and fit-page reader

Complete `src` replacement based on V0.13.49. Keep the existing root configuration and dependencies. No new dependency is required.

## PDF reader

- Default Fit now measures the available reader width **and height**, keeping the complete page visible without stretching its proportions. The paper is centered horizontally and vertically, including Arabic layouts, focus mode, phone landscape and wide two-page spreads.
- Page metadata comes from the existing PDF.js document cache. Extra-tall and mixed-size documents are supported; the empty side of the last spread adopts the visible page's proportions.
- The phone returns to a separate lower page-turn row and a quieter paper surround. Toolbar targets remain compact and touch-friendly. Manual 100%, zoom, drag/pan and Fit remain available.
- The worker, PDF document and fixed source raster are retained through controls, resizing and page changes. Custom arrow tooltips remain in the PDF toolbar.

## Homepage after the hero

- Replaced the vector curiosity road with four illustrated architectural destinations. On wide screens they form a connected campus; phones display larger individual places. Each retains its real CMS label, description and link.
- Replaced the library arches with a detailed library scene, keeping the real 3D leather course books, resources tab and swipe shelf.
- Added an event amphitheatre, opportunity discovery gateway, journal writing room and community courtyard. Event dates and saves, opportunity status and links, article selection and community links remain interactive HTML.
- Six light illustrations and six matching night edits are supplied in two responsive WebP sizes. All assets load locally, lazily and asynchronously. Artwork prompts and filenames are documented in `assets/home-scenes-v1350/ARTWORK.md`.
- Existing scroll reveals, reduced-motion support, Arabic mirroring and theme text colors remain active. The original hero and authentication artwork are unchanged.

## Appearance quick tools

- Hover Appearance for two seconds to reveal language, random theme and navigation orientation controls without shifting the navigation.
- Use the small disclosure on expanded Appearance buttons, hold for 650ms on touch, or focus Appearance and press Arrow Up. Normal click opens the full Appearance panel.
- The tray floats in the browser's top layer, including when opened inside the phone sections menu. Escape and outside interaction dismiss it. Touch movement cancels a pending hold.
- Random themes exclude the current effective choice. Existing guest restrictions remain: MedClub Light/Dark only, with account themes and navigation preferences unlocked after sign-in.

## Validation

Strict TypeScript checking and production Vite build pass. Browser checks cover 144 PDF fit combinations, manual zoom/pan/Fit and page turns without document reopening, both languages, navigation orientations and guest locks. Homepage artwork and layouts are checked at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
